Key Findings
================================================================================

1. Current Mechanism (Source Audit)
   - NanoOWL decode(): threshold filter only, NO NMS, NO top-k
   - Project-side: selects single highest-score patch (top-1)
   - Completely ignores spatial information from other high-score candidates

2. Candidate Spatial Analysis
   - 17 localization failures: 8 cases have better-GT-IoU non-top-1 candidates
   - BUT: these candidates have near-zero pairwise IoU with top-1 (e.g., 0.000-0.084)
   - Candidates are spatially isolated, NOT neighborhood refinements
   - This negates the algorithmic premise of both Fusion and Consensus methods

3. Experimental Results (33-instance frozen eval split)
   
   Metric                    Baseline    Method A (Fusion)    Method B (Consensus)
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Detection success         0.848       0.848                0.848
   Mean Box IoU              0.532       0.479 (-0.054)       0.535 (+0.003)
   Joint Mask IoU            0.407       0.378 (-0.029)       0.407 (+0.001)
   Latency overhead          -           +0.124ms             +0.329ms
   
   - Method A: net negative (6 degraded, 1 improved)
   - Method B: essentially no effect (31 unchanged)
   - 0 localization-propagation cases recovered by either method

4. Root Cause
   - NanoOWL's 576 patches = 24×24 coarse spatial grid (32px per patch)
   - Localization errors are jump to wrong region, NOT slight offset
   - Lacks redundant overlapping candidates (unlike anchor-based detectors)
   - Post-processing methods have no usable signal

5. Next Steps (NOT in v1 scope, requires training resources)
   A. OWL-ViT detection/box head fine-tuning on actual error distribution
   B. Stronger vision-language grounding model (e.g., Grounding DINO)
